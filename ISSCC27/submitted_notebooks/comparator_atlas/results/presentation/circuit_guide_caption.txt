PMOS gates use inversion bubbles; low-threshold input devices are labeled LVT. Filled dots denote electrical junctions; unmarked crossings are unconnected. Identical net labels across panels denote the same electrical node. All NMOS bodies connect to vss and PMOS bodies to vdd; body wires and external 5 fF loads are omitted. W/L values are in micrometres. The schematic is checked against the published transistor netlist.
