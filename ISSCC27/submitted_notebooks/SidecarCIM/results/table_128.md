# 128-row layouts next to the 32-row macros

Generated by `results/make_edge.py` from `results/ppa.csv`, `results/ppa_128.csv`, `results/load_energy.csv`, `python/out/tile_scaling.json` and `python/out/alternatives.json`. The 128-row SidecarCIM is the final build (merge register, 3-bit S) with K = 4 slots per column, the fewest that hold accuracy at this height. W4A4, W6A4 and SidecarCIM were laid out at 35 ns and meet setup and hold at every corner. The SidecarCIM layout is a rerun with the hold-repair margins raised to 0.2 ns (placement) and 0.15 ns (global routing); that is the only setting that differs from the other two. Dense W8A4 did not route at 128 rows, so only its synthesis area exists. Energies are post-layout estimates from real GPT-2 tiles, per MVM at the clock in the table; reload = one full weight load of the macro.

Accuracy on 8 windows (perplexity increase over FP32, GPT-2 / Qwen): dense W6A4 +2.58 / +2.36 at any height; SidecarCIM at 32 rows with k = 2 +1.96 / +1.94; at 128 rows with k = 2 +2.36 / +2.27 and with k = 4 +1.88 / +2.14 (paired t against W6A4 -2.99 / -1.48, against the 32-row design -0.51 / 1.65). These runs used the 4-bit shift encoder; the 3-bit clamp was checked at 32 rows.
