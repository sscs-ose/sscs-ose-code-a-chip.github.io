# Driver design — topology selection and sizing

Date: 2026-10-29 JST; reader-facing text 2026-10-30 JST

The driver was not designed or resized for the sample-and-hold brief of `DESIGN_BRIEF.md`. Conclusions that depend on it (acquisition limited by the driver, little settling effect of the switch width, the class-A sink limit, the static tracking error) are results about this driver.

## 1. What the driver must show

> The driver exists so that the reader can see that, in a real SKY130 transistor circuit, an input-step test with a continuous capacitive load and the recovery after a previously charged sampling capacitor is connected are different tests of the same closed-loop driver, because the two events excite it at different ports and through different internal paths.

Requirements: a clear input; closed-loop feedback; able to drive a switched capacitive load; simple enough to explain; standard SKY130 devices; created from scratch; no exotic biasing. From `../REDUCED_MODEL_ANALYSIS.md`: the driver must contain an internal state that an input step and an output-port charge disturbance excite differently. A driver whose only significant state is its output node approaches the one-state model, for which the two tests coincide.

## 2. Candidates considered

| Candidate | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| single-stage five-transistor OTA in unity-gain feedback | rejected before sizing | in a unity-gain follower the differential input is `Vin − Vout` in both tests and the output node is the only dominant state, so both events reduce to nearly the same slew-then-single-pole recovery |
| **two-stage Miller-compensated amplifier in unity-gain feedback** | **selected** | the first-stage output `x` and the voltage across `Cc` form an internal state: an input step enters through the input pair and must slew `Cc`; a charge disturbance enters at the output, where the second stage and the `Cc`-coupled node `x` respond first. The class-A second stage can source current by raising its gate drive but sinks only its bias current, so recovery may depend on the direction of the disturbance |
| flipped voltage follower | not selected | its output is level-shifted by a gate-source voltage, so the settled output is not a closed-loop copy of the input, and the local loop is less familiar to the intended reader |

The selected topology is the canonical textbook circuit (Razavi 2017; Gray et al. 2009). Nothing is taken from private work.

## 3. Sizing (analytical first sizing, unchanged after DC and AC bring-up)

| Device | Model | W / L (µm) | m | Role and reason |
|---|---|---|---:|---|
| M8 | `nfet_01v8` | 5 / 1 | 1 | diode reference for `Iref` = 10 µA; L = 1 µm for mirror output resistance |
| M5 | `nfet_01v8` | 5 / 1 | 2 | tail, 20 µA |
| M1, M2 | `nfet_01v8_lvt` | 5 / 0.5 | 4 | input pair; LVT and W = 20 µm keep the tail saturated down to an input level near 0.6 V |
| M3, M4 | `pfet_01v8_lvt` | 5 / 0.5 | 2 | mirror load; LVT puts the mirror node near 1.3 V, keeping headroom for inputs up to about 1.2 V |
| M6 | `pfet_01v8_lvt` | 5 / 0.5 | 20 | second stage, 100 µA; `(W/L)6/(W/L)4 = 2 (W/L)7/(W/L)5` for low systematic offset |
| M7 | `nfet_01v8` | 5 / 1 | 10 | second-stage current-source load, 100 µA |
| Cc | ideal | 0.5 pF | – | Miller capacitor; `GBW ≈ gm1 / (2π Cc)` |
| Rz | ideal | 1.5 kΩ | – | moves the right-half-plane zero (`Rz > 1/gm6` ≈ 0.9 kΩ) |

Supply 1.8 V; quiescent current about 130 µA. `Iref`, `Cc`, `Rz` are ideal elements; a bias generator and passive-device layout are outside the scope. The high threshold of the standard `pfet_01v8` (about 1.0 V, see `ENVIRONMENT.md`) is why the input pair and the PMOS devices are LVT.

Bring-up targets: unity-gain stable with 1 pF, phase margin ≥ 60°, loop unity-gain frequency 30–60 MHz. The first sizing met them, and no size, bias, `Cc`, or `Rz` was changed afterwards. Bring-up values (tt, 27 °C, 1 pF): DC tracking within 1 mV for inputs 0.52–1.50 V (+0.43 mV at 0.9 V); loop unity-gain frequency 54 MHz, phase margin 65°, gain margin 23 dB, low-frequency loop gain 55 dB; closed-loop `|Zout|` from 33 Ω at low frequency to a 1.17 kΩ peak near 89 MHz. These are regenerated by `run_sky130.py` (`results/data/sky130_summary.json`, `bringup`).

## 4. Conditional variation

Defined with the sizing: if recovery after a step down (driver sinking charge) is limited by the second-stage NMOS bias current, rerun with M6 and M7 multiplicity doubled (200 µA second stage), `Cc` and `Rz` unchanged (`netlists/source_two_stage_2x.spice`). The condition is evaluated in `run_sky130.py` (`DESIGN_BRIEF.md` Section 5).
