# Attribution and provenance

Original review notebook analysis, evidence exporter and explanatory
content added for this package are provided under Apache-2.0. This is
not a relicensing of the entire AutoMX-CIM development repository.

Bundled SAR RTL is from https://github.com/efabless/SKY130_SAR-ADC1 at revision
`892272208df28b6c7620101e129a9d8dd95ebab7`. Its Apache-2.0 license and existing copyright
headers are retained. SAR/CDAC/comparator circuit work is attributed to
that upstream project. AutoMX-CIM's contribution here is the CIM/readout
integration, experimental protocol, verification and application trace.

SKY130 models/process and standard-cell libraries were inputs to the
native experiment. The top GDS includes SKY130 standard-cell/device
geometry. The corresponding sky130_fd_sc_hd and sky130_fd_pr license
texts are included in licenses/. Process input locations are declared in
standalone.json; the process libraries are distributed in the runtime archive
identified by runtime_assets.json and RELEASE_ASSETS.md. The prepared laboratory
carries process inputs under `pdks/`; their upstream license and attribution
terms continue to apply.
Tool authors retain their respective licenses. See notebook references.

Logs are recorded native measurements. Only host-specific path prefixes
are replaced by descriptive tokens in exported text; numerical rows and
RTL states are unchanged. Bundled SAR source provenance is recorded in
third_party/SKY130_SAR_ADC1/mixed_signal_partition/provenance.json.
The interleaved complete native laboratory preserves its original
source and output contents, including original acquisition paths; those
are recorded historical transcripts, not portable runtime dependencies
for the default review route. Every replayed output is explicitly labeled.
The waveform and GDS preserve their scientific/design contents. Native
review tool output is generated by the visible subprocess calls.

The original two PEX conditions were acquired in the prior verified
native session; two missing factorial conditions and eight challenge
conversions were acquired in the new study. The interrupted first
factorial attempt was retained externally; the completed attempt
supplies the measurements. No challenge result was used for refitting.

The application chapter uses Human Activity Recognition Using Smartphones
from the UCI Machine Learning Repository, credited to Jorge L. Reyes-Ortiz,
Davide Anguita, Alessandro Ghio, Luca Oneto and Xavier Parra:
https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/240/human+activity+recognition+using+smartphones
The dataset is provided under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
The laboratory derives deterministic half-window sensor summaries and trains
its own embedding, attention and classifier models; these modifications are
not an endorsement by the dataset authors. The original data files and
dataset documentation remain under `data/uci_har/` with their attribution.

Clock-aware power measurements use the bundled OpenSTA 3.1.0 build from
https://github.com/parallaxsw/OpenSTA. Its GPL license is retained in
licenses/OpenSTA_LICENSE and its source/build record in tools/opensta_current/.
The statically linked CUDD 3.0.0 (BSD) and Eigen 3.4.0 (MPL2) dependencies
retain their respective licenses. This tool update honors measured VCD clock
activity in the storage-gating comparison; the preceding OpenSTA 2.4 results
remain separate versioned measurements.
