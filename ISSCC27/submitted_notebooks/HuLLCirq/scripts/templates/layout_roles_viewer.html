How to read the colors

Source-confirmed: explicit source or preserved-name evidence, only when the supplied status says so.

Source + connectivity: source evidence confirms a template or function; named nets infer its lane. This is not full source confirmation of that lane.

Net-inferred: supplied semantic net names or recorded backward fan-in attribution. Inferred cones may be shared; they do not reconstruct the original module hierarchy.

Shared / unknown: shared attribution or insufficient evidence remains visible as such.

Physical / clock: implementation-support roles stay distinct from functional block roles.

Colors identify roles. The cell’s status and complete evidence describe the strength and origin of its classification.
